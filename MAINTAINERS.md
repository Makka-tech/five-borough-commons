# Maintainers

Maintainers steward scope, safety, releases, support, and welcoming participation. Selection is behavior-based: examples include mentoring, accessibility review, facilitation, documentation, data validation, conflict care, and sustained project support. A prospective maintainer is nominated publicly, confirms the role and time commitment, and receives community feedback. Removal uses documented concerns, a chance to respond, conflict-of-interest handling, and an appeal when safe. No person is promoted or removed solely for commit counts.
