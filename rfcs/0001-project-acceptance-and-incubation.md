# RFC 0001 — Project acceptance and incubation process

**Status:** Draft (sample governance content, not a decision by nonexistent community members)
**Author:** Five Borough Commons sample content

## Summary

Propose a transparent, resident-informed incubation process.

## Motivation

Prevent premature software commitments and make stewardship visible.

## Proposal

Use the need-triage rubric, publish a scoped recommendation, identify a prospective steward, and seek affected-resident feedback.

## Alternatives

Automatic project creation, private maintainer-only triage, and hackathon acceptance are less accountable alternatives.

## Risks

The process could become burdensome; revise through an RFC if that happens.

## Accessibility impact

Use plain language and asynchronous feedback.

## Privacy impact

Minimize personal data and route sensitive content privately.

## Community impact

Non-software outcomes remain valid and residents receive honest expectations.

## Decision

No decision; sample draft only.

## Decision date

Not decided.
